武士道

In the age of warring states, a lone samurai walked the path of the sword.

At dawn, beneath the crimson sky, he drew his blade and faced the silence.

Steel met steel. Every strike was a lifetime of discipline, every breath a vow.

When the dust settled, only honor remained. The way of the warrior endures.
