No explosion. No warning. Just an empty patch of sky where our oldest companion used to be.

Over the centuries, Earth loses its anchor. Our planet's tilt starts to wander, swinging wildly, and the seasons we know are torn apart.

The nights grow darker than any human has ever seen. The Moon was never just a light in the sky. It was the quiet hand that kept our world steady.
